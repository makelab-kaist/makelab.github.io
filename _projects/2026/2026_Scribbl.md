<!-- Abstract -->

Authoring haptic feedback has traditionally relied on specifying explicit parameters, with recent efforts shifting toward text-based generation. However, bodily expression is often preverbal, text alone is insufficient to capture its subtle nuances or to support rapid, exploratory articulation of as-yet-unnamed sensations. We present _Scribbl_, a tool for _authoring multimodal haptics, built around sketching_. A designer starts from a felt moment, sketches it as a pattern, refines it in words or by hand while feeling it on the body, and collects patterns that are then composed across body-worn actuators into a whole sequence, all without entering explicit parameters or wiring logic.
